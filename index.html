Tips & Tricks

🥖 The bread basket trap

In Lazio the coperto (cover charge) is actually illegal — but tourist-zone waiters drop uninvited bread and snacks on your table. Eat any of it and you'll find €5–€8 on the check.

✅ Just say “No pane, grazie” upfront. The basket disappears, no questions asked.

🗺️ Where Romans actually eat

  • Testaccio — Via Alessandro Volta & Via Luca della Robbia
  • Trastevere — Piazza San Cosimato & Via dei Vascellari (skip the main piazza)
  • Monti — the blocks behind Via Cavour

Italian-only menus, locals at every table, no “tourist menu” signs = you're in the right place.

🍦 Spotting real gelato

  • Covered metal tins or low, dense trays = artisanal. Fluffy neon mountains = industrial.
  • Pistachio should be dull olive-brown, never bright green.
  • All eight gelaterias in this app are the real deal — never more than a 15 min walk away.
